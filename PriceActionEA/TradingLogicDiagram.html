🚀 Quick Start

  1. Copy PriceActionEA.mq5 to MT5/MQL5/Experts/
  2. Compile in MetaEditor (F7)
  3. Open M15 chart of your preferred pair
  4. Drag EA to chart and configure settings
  5. Enable AutoTrading and let it run!
